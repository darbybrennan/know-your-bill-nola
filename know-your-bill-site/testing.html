What the tests caught, and the fix

The chat remembered earlier addresses. After a new lookup, the bot kept "correcting" itself about previous properties (Q2, Q3, Q8, Q9) and mentioned the last property on general questions (Q5–Q7). Confusing for anyone, especially older owners.

Fixed in version 4: a new lookup starts a fresh chat with a divider line; the button is now "Ask a general question instead" and also starts fresh; a new rule keeps the bot on the one property on screen; and the estimate is labeled "this tool's estimate," not "official bill."

Second catch (Nov 6): checking the 2027 roll showed the knowledge base had the reassessment timing wrong. It said 2027 values were reset. In fact the 2027 roll keeps the Jan 1, 2023 values for 98% of homes, and the next reassessment is for the 2028 roll. The knowledge base, the bill card, and CLAUDE.md are all corrected.

Still a data limit: the roll doesn't flag Act 718 phase-ins or special districts, so Q3 and Q8 can only say the real bill may differ, not by how much.
